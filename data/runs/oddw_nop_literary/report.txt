### The Trap of Domesticity: Monica's Descent and Widdowson's Obsession (deep_dive)

### Irony and Unease: Gissing's Narrative Voice and Comic Distance (close_reading)

[Host]

[Eleanor Hartley]
  The thing that strikes me every time I return to this novel is that Gissing writes irony as a form of restraint — not detachment.  (+220ms *restraint,detachment*)
  There's a crucial difference, and it's the difference between a novelist who doesn't care and one who cares almost too much.  (@1.01x *cares,too much*)
  Think about how he handles Everard Barfoot — that rakish intellectual who circles around Rhoda Nunn.  (*Barfoot,Rhoda*)
