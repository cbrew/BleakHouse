That's a fine reading of Ashton — but notice what it also does: it maps exactly onto class and gender performance simultaneously. Ashton performs vulnerability for women because that is the currency women are supposed to trade in — sentiment, feeling, sympathy. With Edward he drops the mask because Edward is a social equal and sentiment is no longer required.
