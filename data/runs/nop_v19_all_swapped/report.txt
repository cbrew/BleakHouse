### Close Reading: Jo and the Machine of Social Indifference (close_reading)

[Host]

[Oliver Trevelyan]

  > "It must be a strange state to be like Jo. To shuffle through the streets, unfamiliar with the shapes, and in utter darkness as to the meaning of those mysterious symbols."  (+150ms @0.93x [reading] *strange state,utter darkness*)

  It's not just illiteracy. It's epistemological exclusion — the world is written in a language Jo was never taught.  (@1.02x *epistemological exclusion*)

[Edmund Leigh]
  Oliver is absolutely right about that gravity, and I want to push on the word a little further.  (+220ms @0.94x *gravity*)

  Dickens is not saying the system failed. He is saying you failed. Every one of you.  (<1200ms> @0.92x *you failed*)

[Daniel Rosen]

  Jo is not an unfortunate exception to the system. He is what the system produces — efficiently, systematically, invisibly.  (@0.99x *produces,invisibly*)
