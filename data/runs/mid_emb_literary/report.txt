### Dorothea's Fatal Idealism: Marriage and Disenchantment (deep_dive)

### Middlemarch Society and Its Hierarchies: Class, Capital, and Corruption (deep_dive)

[Host]

[c40:p45]  (+150ms @0.93x [reading] *choked,mouldering,perfect study*)
  Now, that is genuinely beautiful prose — Eliot is not being sarcastic about the beauty.  [c40:p45]  ([commentary] *genuinely beautiful*)
