### Bastards, Heiresses, and the Law: Social Structures on Trial (deep_dive)

  So when Caroline says Nora has nowhere to put her protest — that's not a failure of nerve on Nora's part. It's an accurate reading of her actual legal position.  (<800ms> @0.96x *accurate,legal position*)

[Eleanor Hartley]

### Magdalen's Mask: Deception as Character (deep_dive)
