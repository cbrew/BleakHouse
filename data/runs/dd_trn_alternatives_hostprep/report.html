Daniel Rosen (marxist_critic)
The right question is: what is the only thing Gwendolen actually has to trade? Oliver calls it theatrical genius. Edmund calls it a poverty of feeling. I want to say it's both those things and something else: it is wit deployed as a survival strategy by a woman who has nothing else on the market. That is not comic genius — that is a woman doing her sums.

Edmund, I love that passage — it's one of the most charged sentences in the novel. But I read it differently — almost opposite to you. I want to come in here, because I think both of you are circling something important without quite landing on it.
