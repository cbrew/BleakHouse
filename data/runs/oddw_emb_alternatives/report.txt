### A Woman's Right to Work: Setting the Stakes (opening)

  Gissing published The Odd Women at a moment when the so-called Woman Question was not an abstraction but a daily crisis for hundreds of thousands of women without income, prospects, or protection.  (@0.97x *Woman Question,crisis*)
