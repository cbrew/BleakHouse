### The Architecture of Influence: Lucilla's Social Mastery (deep_dive)

### Wit, Propriety, and the Absurd: Comedy as Social Commentary (discussion)

[Host]

[Oliver Trevelyan]

  Think of the way she describes Lucilla's evening parties — those famous Thursday evenings that Lucilla has made the social institution of Carlingford.  (*Thursday evenings,institution*)
  She sets it up with such earnestness, as though Lucilla's management of the drawing room is genuinely a matter of civic importance.  (*earnestness,civic*)
  And then the joke arrives — not with a wink, but with a perfectly straight face — and it's twice as funny for it.  (<800ms> @1.02x *straight face*)
  Oliphant writes of Lucilla that she had a great deal of energy and a great deal of good sense, and intended to be a comfort to her dear papa.  (@0.98x [setup])
