### The Echoes Persist: Form, Meaning, and India's Paradox (closing)

[Host]

[Sarah Chen]

  When I parsed the Caves chapters closely, what I found was that pronoun reference becomes genuinely unstable.  (*unstable*)
  Forster writes sentences where 'it' and 'they' and 'something' accumulate without clear antecedents.  (*accumulate*)
  The agent of the action in the cave — whatever happens to Adela — is never syntactically present.  (*agent,syntactically*)

  > 'The echo began in some indescribable way to undermine her hold on life.'  (+150ms @0.93x [reading] *indescribable,undermine*)

[Elena Volkov]
  That's exactly what I keep hearing — but from a different angle.  (+220ms @0.98x)

  The echo in the Marabar is structurally that. It returns every theme the novel has established and drains it of its differentiating content.  (@0.98x *drains,differentiating*)
  Forster tells us the echo says 'boum' — or 'bou-oum' — and that whatever is said to it returns as that sound.  (+120ms @0.97x [setup])
  > 'Pathos, piety, courage — they exist, but are identical, and so is filth.'  (+150ms @0.92x [reading] *identical,filth*)
  That is not a failure of composition. That is a composer putting a rest in the score where listeners expect a chord.  (@0.98x [commentary] *rest*)

[Rebecca Martinez]

  He had tracked everything meticulously for two hundred pages — the light, the social gradations, the heat, who sat where at dinner.  (@0.96x *meticulously*)
