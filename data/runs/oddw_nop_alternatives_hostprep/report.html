Oliver Trevelyan (performer_and_wit)
The register is crisp. Gissing opens with a family inventory — Dr. Madden, six daughters, and a cheerful improvidence that is going to destroy all of them. Edmund Leigh (traditionalist_critic)
I take Oliver's point about the irony, and I won't dispute it. But I'd want to be careful about what we mean by comic.
