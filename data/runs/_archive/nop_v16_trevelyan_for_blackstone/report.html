Eleanor Hartley (literary_critic)
It begins in the architecture of the novel's opening sections — which is to say, it begins with promise deliberately set up to be dismantled. Dickens introduces Richard through Esther's narration, and that's already a structural choice that matters enormously. Esther loves Richard — she's fond of him from the very first chapter at Kenge and Carboy's — and her fondness filters everything we first see.
