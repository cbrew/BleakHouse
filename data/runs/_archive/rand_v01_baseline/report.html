And the bailiff — Coavinses, as he comes to be known — is doing his duty. That is the unbearable thing about the debt enforcement system Dickens is anatomising. The people inside it are not monsters — they are functionaries, following procedures that were, in their way, entirely legal. Skimpole's question — "did you know this morning?" — inadvertently exposes this: the man did know, he came anyway, and that is not a moral failing but a structural one. Dickens never lets us off the hook of that distinction.
