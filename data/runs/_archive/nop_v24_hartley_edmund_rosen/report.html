Eleanor Hartley (literary_critic)
Daniel's point about Jo brings me to something I find genuinely remarkable about the novel's structure — which is that Dickens invented it for this book. Bleak House has two narrators — the third-person omniscient voice that opens the novel, present tense, cold, almost satirical — and Esther Summerson, writing in the first person, past tense, warm and searching. The novel ends with Esther's narration still in the present tense — 'I look,' 'I see,' 'I know' — which means it is never fully retrospective.
