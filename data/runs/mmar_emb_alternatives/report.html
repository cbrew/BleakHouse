Welcome to Miss Marjoribanks Unpacked — a close, unhurried, and genuinely affectionate look at one of the most dazzling comic novels of the Victorian era. Miss Marjoribanks, published in 1866 by Mrs Oliphant, follows Lucilla Marjoribanks — a young woman of formidable intelligence, total self-possession, and quite breathtaking social ambition. She returns from school to her widower father's provincial town, and proceeds — calmly, methodically, brilliantly — to reorganise its entire social life around herself. The novel is a masterwork of irony, of social comedy, and of something far harder to name — a genuine investigation of what intelligence and energy look like when they have nowhere large enough to go.
