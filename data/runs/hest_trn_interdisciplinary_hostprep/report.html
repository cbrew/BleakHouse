Rebecca Martinez
Oliphant has written a novel about a woman with scientific intelligence — pattern recognition, signal detection, model-building — who lives in a social world that has no instrument for measuring that intelligence and therefore records it as zero. The BBC Radio 4 adaptation had to literally insert the outcome Oliphant's form structurally prevents, and that is not an adaptation choice but a diagnosis: the original novel's form is the cage.
Key points:
