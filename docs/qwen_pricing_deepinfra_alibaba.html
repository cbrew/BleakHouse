Qwen pricing — DeepInfra vs Alibaba Model Studio

Snapshot 2026-05-17. Compiled to answer "is OpenRouter cheaper, are other providers cheaper, what's the OpenRouter commission against direct Alibaba?" while picking a routing default for short-format prose generation (BleakHouse-9k9n, dll8 listener panel context). All prices in USD per million tokens.

Sources and what they mean

  • DeepInfra direct — pulled live from https://api.deepinfra.com/models/list. Authoritative; this is the public catalogue endpoint DeepInfra exposes. Pricing fields cents_per_input_token and cents_per_output_token converted with multiplier 1e4 to USD-per-M.
  • Alibaba direct — Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (International / Singapore region) per their pricing page, last updated 2026-04-01. Tiered pricing: Alibaba charges different per-token rates depending on the input-token-count bucket the request falls into. Ranges below are the documented min–max across tiers.
  • Alibaba via OpenRouter — pulled live from https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models/<slug>/endpoints, filtered to provider_name == "Alibaba". OpenRouter exposes a single flat per-token rate per (model, provider) pair; this is what an OR-routed call against the Alibaba backend pays. The spread vs Alibaba direct is the OpenRouter commission plus any tier-collapsing.
Methodology caveat. Alibaba's tiered pricing makes single-number comparison imprecise. The OpenRouter Alibaba price tends to sit at or near the bottom of the published Alibaba tier range, suggesting OR negotiates the lowest tier and adds margin from there. For BleakHouse-shaped traffic (mostly small input, ~3-5k tokens/segment), the bottom-of-tier rate is what we'd actually pay direct, so the OR commission relative to the model's bottom-tier-direct is small (often single-digit percent). Treat any cross-row "commission" inference as approximate.

Headline conclusions

  1. For the dll8-panel winner (Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507), DeepInfra direct is cleanly the cheapest route at $0.071 / $0.100 per M. The Alibaba route (whether direct or via OR) is materially more expensive. No reason to leave DeepInfra for this model.
  2. Alibaba via OpenRouter exposes a wider catalogue — 14 Qwen models that aren't on DeepInfra at all (qwen-plus, qwen3.5-flash, qwen3.5-plus, qwen3.6-flash, qwen3.6-plus, qwen3.6-max-preview, qwen3-coder-flash, qwen3-coder-plus, qwen-plus-2025-07-28, qwen3-30b-a3b-instruct-2507, qwen3-30b-a3b-thinking-2507, qwen3-next-80b-a3b-thinking, qwen3-vl-*-thinking, qwen/qwen3-coder-30b-a3b-instruct). If we want to probe an Alibaba-only model (e.g. qwen3-30b-a3b-instruct-2507 as a cheaper sibling of the panel winner), the OR route is the easiest first call.
  3. OR commission against Alibaba direct appears small or negative. For models where we have both numbers (qwen-plus, qwen3-max), OR's quoted price is at-or-below Alibaba's documented bottom-tier rate. This could be an artefact of (a) OR locking the lowest tier for callers who don't qualify for it on Alibaba direct, or (b) the Alibaba pricing page being slightly stale relative to OR's negotiated rates. Anomaly worth one verification call before relying on the OR Alibaba quote.
  4. Operational implication for BleakHouse-9k9n: for the short-prose default we don't need to introduce a second provider. DeepInfra direct on Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507 stays the recommended pick. The OR-Alibaba route is interesting as a probe channel for models DeepInfra doesn't host, not as a primary route.
